# Kingdom Two Crowns Takeover

**Doom** × Kingdom Two Crowns · visual · idea by the team

Bring Kingdom Two Crowns into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I put Kingdom Two Crowns into Doom: the Greed swarms the map, drops coins, and you pay vagrants to become archers. The coins never stop.

## Install

Put mod.pk3 next to GZDoom and run: gzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file mod.pk3 (or drag mod.pk3 onto gzdoom.exe). You get a Royal Bow; shoot the Greed, grab coins, walk up to vagrants to recruit archers.

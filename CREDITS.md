# Credits
All sprites, textures, sky and sounds were generated for this mod with fal (Nano Banana 2 images, ElevenLabs SFX) and then cut, scaled and tinted in code. Nothing is taken from Kingdom Two Crowns or any commercial game; only its spirit (coins, Greed, archers, vagrants) is borrowed.
Code: original ZScript.
Base game: GZDoom with Freedoom 2 (BSD licence), not included.
